Market Context

As Vietnam’s technology sector continues to grow, companies face increasing challenges in finding qualified tech talent and managing increasingly complex recruitment processes.

60.1% of surveyed employers expect their hiring demand to grow in 2026.

Target Audience

HiTalent is designed for technology companies in Vietnam with recurring recruitment needs, focusing on two collaborating groups:

Recruiters / HR Personnel: Manage job intake, candidate profiles, CV screening, interview coordination and recruitment progress.

Hiring Managers / Interviewers: Define role-specific needs, assess competencies and provide structured interview feedback.

WHERE FRICTION LIVES

Problem Statements

Different roles experience different bottlenecks in the same hiring journey.

HR / Recruiter

  1. Repeated coordination with hiring teams and candidates slows interview scheduling.
  2. Manual CV review and scattered files make tracking and reporting inefficient.
  3. Inconsistent interview forms make feedback difficult to consolidate and compare.

Tech Lead

  1. Unclear technical criteria require repeated clarification with HR.
  2. Project workloads make recruitment requests and feedback easy to miss.
  3. CVs often lack clear evidence of practical contributions, slowing assessment.
